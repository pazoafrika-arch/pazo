How it works
Simple.
Earn forever.
1
Create your account & get your code
Sign up and generate your personal referral code. A unique referral link and QR code are created automatically — ready to share anywhere.
2
Share your link, QR, or code
Someone scans your QR, clicks your link, or enters your referral code when creating their Travela account. They get instant connectivity wherever they travel — no roaming, no plastic cards, no paperwork.
3
They connect. You get paid.
Every time that person buys connectivity through The Travela — now or months from now — you earn a commission. Refer once and keep earning on every purchase they ever make.
